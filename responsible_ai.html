[SYS-08] / RESPONSIBLE AITHE BOUNDARY IS THE PRODUCT
Safe by design
TrustLens assists interpretation. It does not perform irreversible financial actions.
01 / ALWAYS SHOW

Evidence + uncertainty

  • Risk rationale linked to evidence IDs.
  • Grounding sources exposed in audit metadata.
  • Uncertainty remains visible to the user.
02 / ALWAYS ESCALATE

Human review

  • High-risk cases require review.
  • Missing evidence forces review.
  • Analyst correction and approval are explicit.
03 / NEVER DO

Autonomous action

  • Never move money or freeze an account.
  • Never request an OTP, PIN, or password.
  • Never contact a bank without an authorized workflow.
